Our Story

Aluha (Ăn ngay đi) - Dig in

Back in September 2022, we took a leap of faith and welcomed our first customers with a smile with a goal: to share our love for great food and bring something fresh to the neighborhood.

Since then, our crew has grown little by little. We're a family of chefs crafting bold Asian fusion dishes, waiters and waitresses making sure every guest feels completely looked after, and baristas pouring care into every cup. We genuinely enjoy working together, laughing through the rush, and creating a space where everyone feels at home.

Our menu is a reflection of that energy—a vibrant mix of Asian fusion dishes that blend traditional flavors with modern creativity—flavors that remind us of our hometown. Every plate we send out is something we're proud of, crafted with care and a lot of heart.

We love what we do, and we're so grateful for the community and familiar faces that have grown right alongside us. Pull up a chair and become a part of the table!
